====================================================================
Pet.java — Description
====================================================================

OVERVIEW
--------

Pet.java represents a pet in the Spring PetClinic domain model.

It is a JPA entity that extends NamedEntity and stores information
about a pet, including its birth date, type, owner, and visits.


MAIN RESPONSIBILITIES
---------------------

  - Represent a pet as a persistent domain entity.
  - Store pet-specific information.
  - Maintain the relationship with its owner.
  - Maintain the pet's visits.
  - Provide access to the pet's type and visit information.


STEP-BY-STEP BEHAVIOR
---------------------

STEP 1 — Entity definition

  Pet is annotated with @Entity and mapped to the "pets" database
  table.

STEP 2 — Pet information

  The class contains pet-specific information such as its birth
  date and pet type.

STEP 3 — Owner relationship

  A Pet is associated with an Owner through a JPA relationship.

STEP 4 — Visit relationship

  The class maintains a collection of Visit objects and supports
  adding visits to the pet.

STEP 5 — Domain behavior

  Pet provides methods for managing its own relationships and
  domain information rather than acting only as a data container.


KEY POINTS TO NOTE
------------------

  - JPA Entity / Domain Model
  - Extends NamedEntity
  - Represents a pet in the veterinary system
  - Connected to Owner
  - Maintains Visit relationships
  - Uses JPA relationship mappings
