"Our study compares the original human-written Spring PetClinic with an LLM-reconstructed version created through three improved prompts. The LLM gradually improved the architecture by adding clearer layers, Factory abstractions, and Singleton services. The final version preserved both identified design patterns, achieving **100% Pattern Preservation** and **0% Pattern Collapse**. It also increased explicit interfaces from **2 to 7**, giving an **ALS of -2.50**, meaning more abstractions were introduced rather than lost. Overall, the results show that **clear and specific architectural prompts significantly improve LLM architectural fidelity**."
